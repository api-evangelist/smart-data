# SmartData

> SmartData is a hosted drone-data workspace built by National Drones (Brisbane,
> Australia). Teams bring processed, viewer-ready outputs from DJI Terra, Agisoft
> Metashape or their own photogrammetry workflow and host them in SmartData so clients
> and asset teams can inspect, measure, compare and sign off on the data in a browser
> instead of passing files around. It serves power and utilities, telecommunications,
> mining and resources, forestry and environment, and infrastructure and construction.

**SmartData publishes no public API, no developer portal, no SDK and no
machine-readable contract of any kind.** Do not attempt to integrate with it
programmatically. Its only machine surface is the session-authenticated backend the
web application calls at `https://app.ndsmartdata.com/api/v1/`, which is undocumented,
unmarketed, and returns HTTP 403 to anonymous callers. Treat SmartData as a
human-operated SaaS product, not an API provider. To discuss integration, contact
the company directly.

## Product

- [SmartData platform](https://www.smart-data.com/software/): the whole suite - hosted datasets, inspection AI, 2D/3D review, reporting, team handoff

## Supported upload packages

SmartData hosts processed, viewer-ready packages only: orthomosaic hosted views,
Cesium 3D Tiles, tiled point cloud packages, and packaged Gaussian Splat hosted views.
Raw DJI or Agisoft project folders, raw photos, loose GeoTIFF, loose LAS/LAZ, loose
OBJ/FBX and arbitrary file drops are NOT direct BYO uploads and are quoted separately
as conversion or processing work.

## Pricing (published, AUD)

Metered by hosted dataset count and seats. Storage is a stated fair-use allowance;
overage is reviewed manually rather than billed automatically.

- BYO Dataset Pass - AUD 149/year, 1 hosted dataset, 50 GB, 2 seats
- BYO Starter - AUD 249/year, 3 hosted datasets, 150 GB, 5 seats
- BYO Pro - AUD 249/month, 15 hosted datasets, 500 GB, 10 seats
- BYO Enterprise - custom, 5 TB baseline, custom datasets and seats, contact-led
